Our Journey

The Story of Mahakali Gaurav Travels

Founded with a strong commitment to redefining passenger travel, Mahakali Gaurav Travels started its journey to provide safe, comfortable, and reliable transportation solutions across Maharashtra.

What began as a passion for quality service has grown into a comprehensive mobility company trusted by thousands of passengers, corporate enterprises, educational institutions, and holiday travelers. From our inception, we identified a crucial gap in the transport industry: the need for reliable, punctual, and transparent travel services backed by modern vehicles and verified staff.

"Our core philosophy is simple: Put passenger safety, vehicle comfort, and operational reliability at the center of everything we do."

Over the years, we have continuously expanded our service offerings to meet the evolving demands of our clients. Today, Mahakali Gaurav Travels operates a modern, versatile fleet including luxury AC sleeper and seater buses, executive sedans, spacious SUVs, Tempo Travellers, and premium corporate shuttles.

Whether managing large-scale daily corporate employee transportation for MNCs, facilitating seamless intercity bus routes between major industrial hubs, or curating unforgettable holiday tour packages across India, Mahakali Gaurav Travels remains dedicated to delivering excellence on every single mile.
