Our Story
Our Journey

The Story of Mahakali Travels

Building a legacy of trust, safety, and excellence in passenger transportation.

Mahakali Travels was founded with a clear, unwavering vision: to transform road travel across Maharashtra into a comfortable, safe, and premium experience for every passenger.

Starting as a modest fleet operator dedicated to local route services, our journey began with a commitment to customer service and absolute punctuality. Recognizing the growing demand for reliable corporate employee transport and luxury long-distance travel, we systematically expanded our fleet to include modern air-conditioned buses, corporate coaches, executive sedans, and SUVs.

Over the years, Mahakali Travels has earned the trust of over 500 top-tier corporate clients, educational institutions, and thousands of family travelers. Our growth has been fueled by continuous investment in driver safety training, vehicle maintenance technologies, and 24/7 client support systems.

Today, whether executing daily shift transportation for corporate workforces or curating memorable pilgrimage and holiday tour packages across India, Mahakali Travels stands as a benchmark of reliability, safety, and operational excellence.
