1. Overview

Mahakali Tours & Travels ("we", "our", or "us") is a premier travel service provider based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, offering bus route bookings, cab rentals, corporate employee transit solutions, and tour packages. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal details when you interact with our website, book services via WhatsApp, or communicate with our team.

2. Information We Collect

To provide smooth travel bookings and customer support, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Details: Name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Travel Information: Pickup and drop locations, departure dates, number of passengers, and vehicle preferences.
  • Corporate Transport Data: Company name, employee transit requirements, and route preferences for corporate inquiries.
  • Communication Logs: Message content submitted via our contact forms or direct WhatsApp bookings.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business and customer service purposes:

  • Processing bus seat reservations, private cab bookings, and tour packages.
  • Sending instant booking confirmations and trip updates directly via WhatsApp or Phone call.
  • Providing customized corporate mobility quotes and employee transport schedules.
  • Improving our fleet management, web interface, and customer assistance.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard administrative and technical security measures to ensure your data is kept safe from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

5. Third-Party Services

When you initiate direct booking requests via WhatsApp, your interaction is subject to WhatsApp's standard terms and privacy practices. For statutory compliance or highway safety regulations (such as permit generation), required vehicle passenger manifests may be submitted to relevant transport authorities as prescribed by government laws.
