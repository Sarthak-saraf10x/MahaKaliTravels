1. Acceptance of Terms

By reserving a ticket for daily route buses, booking a private vehicle from Our Fleet, reserving a tour package, or contracting corporate transit solutions through Mahakali Tours & Travels ("Agency"), you acknowledge and agree to comply with the terms and conditions outlined below.

2. Booking & Confirmation Policy

All service bookings are processed subject to seat or vehicle availability:

  • Bus Routes & Cab Bookings: Bookings confirmed via WhatsApp or Phone call are binding upon issuance of booking confirmation.
  • Fare Structure: Cab and rental fleet rates are billed on a per-kilometer basis (₹/km) or fixed trip package rates as quoted during booking confirmation.
  • Valid Identification: Passengers must carry a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Driving License, Voter ID) during travel for verification.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

In case of trip cancellation requests by the customer, the following standard guidelines apply:

  • 24+ Hours Before Departure: Eligible for full or partial credit transfer towards future travel.
  • Within 12 Hours of Departure: Cancellation charges may apply based on vehicle reservation costs incurred.
  • Agency Cancellation: If a trip is cancelled due to unforeseen mechanical, weather, or highway emergencies, Mahakali Travels will offer full refunds or alternate transport arrangements.

4. Passenger Responsibilities & Conduct

To ensure safe, comfortable journeys for all passengers and corporate clients:

  • Punctuality: Passengers on daily bus routes are requested to arrive at boarding points at least 15 minutes prior to scheduled departure.
  • Luggage & Safety: Personal belongings remain the responsibility of the passenger. Hazardous, flammable, or illegal items are strictly prohibited on all vehicles.
  • Conduct: Courteous behavior towards co-passengers and drivers is mandatory. Smoking and consumption of alcohol on vehicles are strictly prohibited.

5. Toll, Taxes & Parking Fees

For private cab and bus rental services, state entry permits, toll gate charges, parking fees, and driver allowances (for multi-day outstation trips) will be specified clearly in the initial booking quote or charged at actuals based on route selection.
